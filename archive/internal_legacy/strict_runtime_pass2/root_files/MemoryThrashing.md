  The Logic: 100.0 / RPS

  Examples:

  100.0 / 1 = 100 seconds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
  → clamped to max(5, min(30, 100)) = 30 seconds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  → Bursts every 30 seconds (infrequent)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  Medium traffic service (RPS = 10 req/s):                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  100.0 / 10 = 10 seconds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
  → max(5, min(30, 10)) = 10 seconds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  → Bursts every 10 seconds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  High traffic service (RPS = 50 req/s):                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  100.0 / 50 = 2 seconds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  → clamped to max(5, min(30, 2)) = 5 seconds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  → Bursts every 5 seconds (very frequent)

  → Still bursts every 5 seconds (floor limit)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  Why Inverse Scaling?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  The reasoning is:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - High traffic service: Needs frequent bursts to show up in metrics (many requests per second, so burst needs to affect many requests)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  - Low traffic service: Can have infrequent bursts (few requests per second, so even rare bursts will affect multiple requests)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  The Clamping (5-30 seconds)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Min 5 seconds: Prevents thrashing too fast (would be unrealistic)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
  - Max 30 seconds: Prevents bursts being too rare (wouldn't show up well in metrics)

  burst_period_sec = base_period_sec * (1.5 - severity)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Severity = 0.0 → multiply by 1.5 (longer period, less frequent)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Severity = 0.5 → multiply by 1.0 (keep base period)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  - Severity = 1.0 → multiply by 0.5 (shorter period, more frequent)
